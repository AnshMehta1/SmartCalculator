# Design Thinking

## C1.1 Empathize

1. What information do you expect before applying for a loan?
2. What makes the loan application process confusing or frustrating?
3. Would you like suggestions to improve your eligibility if your application is rejected?

## C1.2 Define

How might we help customers quickly understand whether they are eligible for a loan and provide clear reasons and guidance to improve their eligibility?

## C1.3 Ideate

### Approach 1

Implement a rule-based eligibility engine using predefined banking policies on income, existing debt, and requested loan amount.

### Approach 2

Integrate with a credit scoring and financial analytics service to provide personalized eligibility decisions and recommendations.
