# Requirements

## Functional Requirements

1. The system shall accept the customer's monthly income.
2. The system shall accept the customer's existing loan amount.
3. The system shall accept the requested loan amount.
4. The system shall determine loan eligibility using bank rules.
5. The system shall display the eligibility decision with reasons.

## Non-functional Requirements

1. The eligibility result shall be generated within 2 seconds.
2. Customer financial data shall be encrypted during transmission and storage.
3. The system shall be available 99.9% of the time.

## Out of Scope

1. The feature will not approve or disburse loans.
2. The feature will not calculate credit scores from external agencies.
