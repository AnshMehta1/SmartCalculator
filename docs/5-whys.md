# 5 Whys Analysis

## Problem

Customers are abandoning the loan form on step 2.

### Why 1

Because they stop filling in the form.

### Why 2

Because they do not understand the requested financial information.

### Why 3

Because the form lacks explanations and examples.

### Why 4

Because usability guidance was not included during design.

### Why 5

Because user research and usability testing were insufficient before release.

## Root Cause

Insufficient user-centered design and guidance.

## Actionable Fix

Add field explanations, tooltips, examples, and perform usability testing before release.
